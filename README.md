# L3_T1_310_PCM
Design of Sampler, Quantizer, and Encoder Scheme for Pulse Code Modulation

A popular method for digitizing analog signals, pulse code modulation (PCM), finds 
utility in audio recording, telecommunications, and many other industries. The sampler, 
quantizer, and encoder – three basic parts that are necessary for PCM systems – are the 
main subjects of this project's design and execution. The continuous-time analog signal 
must be discretized by the sampler into a series of discrete amplitude values. The 
technique of transferring each sampled amplitude value into a limited number of discrete 
levels is known as quantization. The quantized amplitude values are transformed by the 
encoder into a binary representation that may be stored or transmitted. This project 
investigates design strategies for every part, taking into account variables like 
computational complexity, quantization levels, encoding methods, and sampling rate. To 
assess the performance of the designed methods in terms of signal accuracy and 
efficiency, simulations and real-world implementations will be carried out.
